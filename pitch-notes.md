# Chris Cenac Jr. Store Prototype

## Positioning

This concept is a standalone player page with a connected store, not a shared
marketplace. The backend playbook can be reused for other clients, but each
athlete should have their own storefront, brand language, player story, product
line, email list, and customer path.

## Direction

- Player-first landing page with store attached
- Luxury streetwear, not generic fan merch
- Dark cinematic visual system with red, cream, steel, and gold accents
- Mobile-first flow for Instagram and Link.me traffic
- Drop-based product strategy, starting with a small capsule
- STRIVE content used as the story engine around the store

## Page Flow

- Hero: Chris Cenac Jr. identity and quick player positioning
- Highlight strip: gold medal, Houston, New Orleans, STRIVE
- Player profile: context before commerce
- Story timeline: New Orleans, national attention, Houston chapter
- Media: STRIVE, announcements, training content
- Store: featured capsule and category filters
- Connect: email capture, social, and business contact

## First Capsule

- CENAC 5 Hoodie
- Pressure Tee
- Gold Medalist Tee
- Icon Sticker Pack

## Upgrade Path After Pitch

- Replace prototype screenshots with clean approved photography
- Add real product mockups and sizing
- Connect Shopify checkout
- Connect Printful or Printify fulfillment
- Add email/SMS capture
- Add analytics and retargeting pixels
- Duplicate the structure for future clients as separate stores
